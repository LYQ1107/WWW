# Reviewer novelty and attribution risks

The research claim is conditional, not established by Phase V/VI or an early positive Phase VII
score. Current MATCH has semantic action scoring, not explicit candidate identity value scoring.

1. **Solver confounding.** A learned rejection can unlock a globally better assignment. Rules,
   ordinary MLPs and binary heads must have that same operation and second validation. B2
   VALIDATION_ONLY identifies a conditional global effect, not superiority over other policies.
2. **Representation and optimization confounding.** Identical 64-state inputs and parameter totals
   still permit normalization differences. Retain the preregistered normalization-matched MLP
   and disclose collapsed policies without generalizing from one seed/objective.
3. **Inherited supervision.** Historical B2 targets contain prefix-reset/frozen-action continuation.
   Native commits do not retroactively make those labels native counterfactual truth. Compare
   supervision separately and require exact native factual/control replay for new records.
4. **Existing lifecycle/association work.** The related-work matrix documents earlier MDP policies,
   learned trajectory ID prediction, occupancy/newborn handling, cooperative candidate association
   and confidence-based memory updates. Enum action count and adapter sharing are insufficient
   novelty. Candidate-conditioned ordinary MLP/DeepSets must remain competitive baselines.
5. **Unavailable commercial architecture.** The project implements a small typed scorer inspired
   by a decision interface. It does not reproduce proprietary Jev, and CE plus temperature is not
   rationale-conditioned RLCD. Language benchmark calibration does not transfer as a tracking theorem.
6. **Missing opportunities.** Correct identity absent from active/stale candidates cannot be fixed
   by a better selector. Forced bad branches and majority ACCEPT labels alone do not qualify a
   candidate-value dataset. Unknown GT, ties, ESS and bin coverage must remain explicit.
7. **Lifecycle production mismatch.** Relative REACT exists in research adapters but not production
   hooks. No complete three-question native production parity or trained Unified model exists.
   MEMORY ties require bank/read attribution, not larger heads or invented nonzero rewards.
8. **Evaluation scope.** TRAIN controller holdout differs from backbone-independent testing.
   Preserve per-sequence harms and true pooled TrackEval; three videos do not establish statistical
   significance. Budget quotas alter trigger timing and are diagnostic, not deployment policies.
9. **Temporal and WWW claims.** Fixed identity mappings and observed frame episodes must replace
   ambiguous wrong-observation counts labelled seconds. Reproducible identity-index retrieval can
   connect association to false joins/contamination; network-video input alone does not prove WWW scope.

WHAT DID WE LEARN? The smallest defensible revision is to establish mechanism attribution and
native data opportunity first. Architecture expansion and shared lifecycle training remain subject
to explicit data/transition gates, even when a pooled tracking score is positive.
